## Design Context

### Users
DCS World flight simulator players and pilots. They use this tool during or between flight sim sessions to research aircraft systems, procedures, weapons employment, and avionics. Context is focused, task-oriented — they need answers fast to get back in the cockpit.

### Brand Personality
**Precise. Efficient. Purposeful.**
The interface should feel like a well-designed instrument — every element earns its place, nothing decorative without function. Think flight planning tools, not social media.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Tone**: Minimal & fast — utilitarian precision with warmth
- **Theme**: Both dark and light modes. Neutrals are tinted warm (amber/sand) to evoke cockpit instrument lighting; the accent is a jade/green that contrasts intentionally against the warm neutrals — readable, calm, and distinctly *not* the AI neon-cyan default.
- **Typography**: Technical but readable — geometric headings, humanist body
- **Layout**: Clean single-column chat with collapsible session sidebar
- **Signature**: Subtle radar-sweep animation as the "thinking" indicator — small, purposeful, distinctly aviation
- **Anti-references**: Generic ChatGPT clone, neon-on-dark AI aesthetic, glassmorphism, gradient text

### Design Principles
1. **Every pixel earns its place** — No decoration without function. If it doesn't help the pilot find answers, remove it.
2. **Speed over spectacle** — Fast interactions, streaming responses, zero loading friction.
3. **Warm precision** — Technical doesn't mean cold. Amber-tinted neutrals make the tool feel crafted, not clinical; the jade accent reads against them as a calm "go" signal.
4. **Context over chrome** — The research content is the hero, not the UI around it. Maximize reading comfort.
5. **Distinctly aviation** — Subtle nods to the domain (radar sweep, instrument-like details) without cosplaying as a HUD.
